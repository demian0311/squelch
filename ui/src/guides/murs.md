# MURS

Five VHF channels near 151 and 154 MHz for anyone, no licence, 2 W. Store staff, farms, hunting camps and a few families.

## What you will hear

- Big-box store employees ("price check on aisle 9"): many chains run their headsets on 154.570 and 154.600, which is why those two are called Blue Dot and Green Dot after the old Motorola radios.
- Farms, construction sites, hunting parties, and preppers who like a licence-free VHF.
- Range a mile or two between handhelds, more from a base antenna.

## How this radio reads it

Narrow FM. The 151 MHz channels are narrow (11.25 kHz); the 154 MHz pair may be wider (20 kHz). The two groups are 2.7 MHz apart, more than one capture, so the Channels panel shows one side at a time.

## Rules

- [FCC Part 95 subpart J](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/part-95/subpart-J). No licence to listen or to talk.
- 2 W. No repeaters.
- Business use is allowed, which is why stores are here.
